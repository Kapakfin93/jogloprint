## Brand & Style

The design system establishes an identity for a high-quality local digital printing house rooted in Demak, Central Java. The design language marries the grounded warmth and architectural heritage of traditional Javanese *joglo* structures with the precision, crispness, and clarity of modern commercial production.

### Brand Personality & Emotional Tone
- **Terpercaya & Tangguh (Reliable & Robust):** Reassuring customers that their banners, packaging, marketing collateral, and official prints will turn out sharp, color-accurate, and on time.
- **Hangat & Ramah Lokal (Warm & Locally Rooted):** Accessible and welcoming to MSME owners (*pelaku UMKM*), event organizers, students, and municipal institutions across Demak and surrounding regions.
- **Rapi & Presisi (Clean & Professional):** Every layout, specification table, and price breakdown communicates clarity and order, eliminating guesswork without resorting to bloated e-commerce tropes.

### Visual Style
The visual style is **Contemporary Tactile Modernism**. It prioritizes generous warm negative space, confident structural containers, fine hairline division lines, and earthy editorial accents. Rather than behaving like an impersonal online mega-store, the interface acts as an interactive digital product catalog and order consultation conduit—guiding the user toward direct communication via WhatsApp and local workshop visits.

## Colors

The palette draws directly from the artisanal materials of Central Javanese craftsmanship—terracotta roof tiles, natural timber, golden ochre dyes—counterbalanced by functional ink tones and high-conversion communication accents.

### Palette Breakdown
- **Primary (`#C2410C` Deep Terracotta):** Anchors primary brand actions, header badges, active tab markers, and dominant visual weight. Represents the fired clay bricks of Demak architecture and the heat of the printing press. Deep hover variant: `#9A3412`.
- **Secondary (`#D97706` Warm Amber / Gold Ochre):** Used for highlighting technical specifications, premium finishing callouts (e.g., UV spot, hot stamping gold foil), and category markers.
- **Tertiary / Action Accent (`#16A34A` Emerald WhatsApp Green):** Reserved exclusively for communication pathways, order consultation triggers, instant quotation chats, and operational status indicators (e.g., "Buka Hari Ini").
- **Neutral Canvas (`#FAF9F6` Soft Warm Off-White / Cream):** Replaces clinical sterile white for the page foundation, eliminating harsh glare and imparting a tactile paper-like warmth.
- **Surface Containers (`#FFFFFF` Crisp White):** Houses individual card items, spec matrices, and calculation tables with sharp distinction against the cream backdrop.
- **Text & Structural Borders:**
  - Headings & Deep Ink: `#1E293B` (Warm Slate/Charcoal)
  - Body & Secondary Copy: `#475569`
  - Subtle Labels & Meta: `#64748B`
  - Hairline Dividers & Card Outlines: `#E2E8F0` / `#E7E5E4`

## Typography

The type system is powered entirely by **Plus Jakarta Sans**, providing crisp geometric contours, generous counters, and humanist curves. This delivers legibility on dense technical specifications (GSM weights, sheet dimensions, minimum order quantities) while retaining an approachable, modern Indonesian identity.

### Structural Typographic Rules
- **Display & Headlines:** Set in weights `700` and `800` with tight letter spacing (`-0.02em` to `-0.01em`) to create an authoritative, print-editorial presence for product categories and value propositions.
- **Price Tiers & Dimensional Specs:** Numbers in pricing tables and dimension indicators (`m²`, `gsm`, `pcs`) utilize tabular figures (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`) to maintain alignment across rows and tiers.
- **Badges & Overlines:** Set in `label-sm` with `uppercase` transform and subtle letter tracking (`+0.05em`) to distinguish material finishes, lead times, and starting price indicators.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model is built on an adaptable **12-column responsive grid** anchored to a maximum container width of `1200px` for desktop screens, ensuring clean presentation without excessive horizontal drift.

### Responsive Breakpoints & Container Logic
- **Desktop (>= 1024px):** 12 columns, `1.5rem` (`24px`) gutters, `2rem` (`32px`) page margin. Product catalogs render in 3 or 4-column cards. Specification breakdowns sit side-by-side with material previews.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8 columns, `1.25rem` (`20px`) gutters, `1.5rem` (`24px`) page margin. Two-column product grid with sticky filter chip rail.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns, `1rem` (`16px`) gutters, `1.25rem` (`20px`) margin. Single-column card stack with horizontal kinetic-scroll ribbons for category selector chips.

### Spacing Cadence
- Use `space-xs` (4px) and `space-sm` (8px) for badge insets, icon-to-label gaps, and pill counters.
- Use `space-md` (16px) for interior component padding (card headers, chip clusters, input padding).
- Use `space-lg` (24px) for card body cushioning, grid gap vertical rhythm, and drawer interiors.
- Use `space-xl` (40px) to delineate distinct operational modules: Category Overview, Material Explorer, Volume Price Matrix, and Demak Workshop Location.

## Elevation & Depth

To reflect the tactile nature of physical paper stocks, corrugated boards, and vinyl fabrics, the design system utilizes **Tonal Layers with Soft Ambient Diffusions** rather than heavy drop shadows or harsh skeuomorphism.

### Depth Hierarchy
1. **Level 0 (Flat / Canvas):**
   The base background tone (`#FAF9F6`). Un-elevated, non-clickable areas.
2. **Level 1 (Card & Container Surface):**
   `#FFFFFF` crisp panels positioned over the warm canvas. Outlined by a gentle hairline border (`1px solid #E2E8F0`) paired with an ultra-subtle warm shadow: `0 1px 3px rgba(30, 41, 59, 0.04), 0 1px 2px rgba(30, 41, 59, 0.02)`.
3. **Level 2 (Hovered Cards & Interactive Drops):**
   Product items on hover rise slightly (`transform: translateY(-2px)`) with an expanded ambient terracotta-tinted blur: `0 10px 20px -5px rgba(194, 65, 12, 0.08), 0 4px 6px -2px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.03)`.
4. **Level 3 (Floating WhatsApp Concierge & Sticky Navigation):**
   Elevated utility layer with high visual contrast. Shadow: `0 12px 28px -4px rgba(22, 163, 74, 0.25), 0 4px 10px -2px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.06)`. Ensures the primary consultation channel remains permanently discoverable above all page content.

## Shapes

The design system enforces a **Rounded Modern** geometry (`roundedness: 2`), reflecting precision-cut paper and smooth laminate finishes while preserving friendliness.

### Radius Scale
- **Small Controls (`rounded`, 6px - 8px):** Form controls, dropdown selectors, volume tier chips, and nested spec cells.
- **Cards & Banners (`rounded-lg`, 16px):** Catalog product cards, price matrix tables, and location info tiles.
- **Overlays & Floating Triggers (`rounded-xl`, 24px):** Quick-view sheets, material guides, and regional workshop callout containers.
- **Pills (`rounded-full`, 9999px):** Status badges ("Bahan Ready"), "Mulai dari" price tags, category selector filters, and the Floating WhatsApp FAB.

## Components

This interface operates strictly as a showcase catalog and consultation conduit. Per system rules, there are **no shopping carts, checkout forms, user authentication flows, or star rating widgets**. Every touchpoint directs the user toward clear specifications and direct WhatsApp inquiry.

### 1. Buttons
- **Primary Order Inquiry Button:** Deep Terracotta (`#C2410C`) background, pure white text, semi-bold weight, `rounded-lg`. Hover state shifts to `#9A3412`. Accompanied by a chat or spec icon.
- **Direct WhatsApp Action Button:** Solid Emerald Green (`#16A34A`), crisp white label, with standard WhatsApp glyph. Hover transitions to `#15803D`.
- **Secondary / Spec Details Button:** Warm Slate border (`1.5px solid #CBD5E1`), background transparent or `#FFFFFF`, text `#1E293B`.

### 2. Product Catalog Cards
- Pure white container with `rounded-lg` and `1px solid #E2E8F0`.
- **Image / Preview Aspect:** Fixed 4:3 or 1:1 image area showcasing realistic print output with subtle interior padding.
- **Price Badge:** Prominently displays "Mulai dari" followed by large tabular figures (e.g., `Mulai dari Rp15.000 / m²`) in bold terracotta text.
- **Specs Summary:** Includes compact metadata tags (e.g., "Flexi Korea 440g", "Outdoor", "Cetak 1 Hari").
- **Card CTA:** Full-width "Konsultasi Cetak" button that opens WhatsApp pre-populated with that specific product's name.

### 3. Price Tier Table (Tabel Grosir / Volume Pricing)
- Clear structured matrix showing quantity breaks (e.g., 1–50 pcs, 51–200 pcs, >200 pcs).
- Highlight row for "Paling Populer" or "Rekomendasi UMKM" using a subtle amber tint background (`#FEF3C7`) with amber border (`#F59E0B`).
- Clean horizontal divider borders with zero clutter, emphasizing cost-efficiency at volume.

### 4. Selector Chips & Spec Dropdowns
- **Chips:** Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), inactive state is neutral off-white with `#E2E8F0` border; active state is `#C2410C` fill with white text.
- **Dropdowns:** Custom minimalist select inputs used for choosing finishing (e.g., "Mata Ayam", "Selongsong", "Laminasi Doff/Glossy") with bold, legible typography and high touch-target heights (min 44px).

### 5. Floating WhatsApp Concierge (FAB)
- Anchored to the bottom-right corner (`bottom: 24px; right: 24px; z-index: 50`).
- Features a dual layout: an icon pill expanding into "Tanya Cetak Sekarang" on desktop hover, resting as an accessible high-contrast green circular badge on mobile.
- Includes a subtle pulsing green indicator indicating live operational hours at the Demak workshop.

### 6. Local Demak Workshop & Footer Module
- Built to engender local trust and walk-in confidence.
- Displays an integrated workshop address block (e.g., "Jl. Sultan Fatah / Lingkar Demak"), clear store operating hours, pickup/delivery coverage (Demak, Kudus, Semarang timur), and a direct "Buka di Google Maps" link.
- Emphasizes physical presence and direct payment options upon proof approval without digital cart complexity.
